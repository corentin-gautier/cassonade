# Sound credits

The sounds in `recorded/` are cut from these recordings on Wikimedia Commons, Freesound and the National Park Service: converted to mono, trimmed, filtered (the cuckoo and owl also isolated from their background, the owl then given a soft reverb) and leveled, by `tools/recorded_sounds.py`. Each is shared under its source's license. The other sounds were synthesized for this mod.
