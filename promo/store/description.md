## Less friction, more life.

**Cassonade** is a vanilla+ quality-of-life mod for **Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric**. It fixes the hundred small annoyances of survival and makes the world feel alive, with birds, waves and starry nights, and towns and treasures worth the trip. No new ores, no magic, no machines: it's the same game you know, just smoother and a lot less empty.

**[Website](https://cassonademod.com/)** · **[Source code](https://github.com/corentin-gautier/cassonade)** · **[Report a bug](https://github.com/corentin-gautier/cassonade/issues)**

## The idea

I made this mod because even though I love Minecraft, I'm usually frustrated by it. The worlds feel empty and pointless, exploration is not really rewarded, some mechanics are really annoying, it feels punishing overall and I clearly don't want to get a PhD to master redstone.

I'm also not a fan of modpacks because they add way too many settings everywhere, each with their own style and some mods just don't "feel" like Minecraft to me making it a sort of weird chimera in my mind. I love coherence and simplicity, so it's just not an option for me even though they add a lot of otherwise very good things.

In real life I'm a web designer, so I want stuff to be beautiful, coherent, logical and practical for the users, and recently I was invited to play on a server with a huge modpack and it just didn't feel right. So I made the one I wanted to play:

*   **Same progression.** I didn't add any new resources, tiers or dimensions. Every new tool is unlocked by equipping an item you already know (a compass, a clock) or one crafted from vanilla items (a backpack, a world map, a barometer), in its own equipment slot.
*   **Every small annoyance, fixed.** Leaves that take forever to decay, "Too Expensive!", boats stuck on a pixel, the enchanting table's "…?": each fix is small, but together they really change how the game feels.
*   **Feels like it was always there.** The interface keeps Minecraft's pixel style and sounds, tools only show up once you equip their item, and you can remap every key.
*   **A world that feels alive.** Flocks cross the sky, waves break on the shore, crickets sing through warm nights. Nothing to fight, collect or craft, just what makes a place feel inhabited.
*   **A world worth exploring.** That's my answer to "exploration is not really rewarded": towns, taverns, lighthouses on the coasts, furnished mansions, temples with real treasures, a pirate's booty hidden in a lush cave. Going out there finally pays off. And less friction doesn't mean less challenge: the sea is murky and dark now, so a dive is a real trip you get ready for.
*   **One style, no settings maze.** That's exactly my problem with modpacks: a dozen mods means a dozen visual styles and as many config screens. Cassonade looks the same everywhere and there's nothing to configure, on purpose: each feature works one way, as close to vanilla as possible.

## Features

### [Backpack](https://cassonademod.com/features/backpack/)

54 more slots, as much as a large chest, crafted from 4 leather, 4 string and a bundle.

*   It goes in its own equipment slot, and you open it with `B` or from a tab at the top of the inventory, in a leather panel where the recipe book usually sits.
*   Shift-click sends items straight into the open backpack.
*   Like a Shulker box, it keeps its contents when you place it in a chest or drop it.
*   **Organized backpack**: a rare smithing template, found in ancient city, bastion treasure and end city chests, and now and then in pillager outpost, desert pyramid, jungle temple, igloo, ocean monument and pirate booty chests, upgrades it with a hopper. Then it refills your hand, feeds the recipe book, and tops up its own stacks with what you pick up.

### [A living world](https://cassonademod.com/features/living-world/)

This is my answer to the empty worlds. It's always there, there's nothing to equip, and none of it changes the game.

*   **In the sky**: flocks of geese and crows, white gulls near the coast, rarely a great murmuration of starlings at dusk; a deeper blue by day, a faint Milky Way, shooting stars and auroras by night, silent heat lightning on warm evenings.
*   **In the air and on the water**: rain the color of water, drops running down windows and frost creeping over them in the cold, blizzards and sandstorms that lay sand on the dunes, butterflies around flowers, robins on the tree tops, sparrows on fences and roofs, swallows around a nest under the eaves, squirrels on the tree trunks, scorpions in the desert sand, cats sleeping by day and hunting spiders at night, drifting pollen, dust falling in caves, morning mist, your breath in the cold, fish leaping at dawn and dusk, a line of foam running up the shore, banks of glowing plankton in warm seas on some nights, water splashing at the foot of falls.
*   **Creepers**: they always looked off next to the newer mobs to me, so now they're a small bush on four legs, with leaves and branches sticking out, a bit different on each one, and a dark wood ribcage on their chest that glows when they're about to blow.
*   **What you hear**: the sea all around you as you near a shore, robins, cuckoos and owls in the trees, frogs in the swamps, crickets through warm nights, grasshoppers in savannas, the wind of a storm, and the world muffled under water.

### [Light and sky](https://cassonademod.com/features/graphics/)

The same blocks in a better light, and you don't need a shader pack.

*   **Colored lights**: each light tints what it lights with its own color, soul fire blue, redstone red, copper green, amethyst purple; the sun washes it out by day. A torch in hand lights up around you.
*   **The sky**: a deep blue that pales towards the horizon, distant land melting into it rather than into white.

### [Compass minimap](https://cassonademod.com/features/minimap/)

Equip a compass and you get a minimap in the top-right corner, drawn with the colors of vanilla maps.

*   Zoom from 32 to 256 blocks across, north-up or turning with you.
*   The biome and your coordinates right underneath.
*   A direction bar that shows where your markers are and names the one you're aiming at, and each marker in the world, through terrain, with its name and distance once you're close.
*   In the Nether, the map shows the floor you're standing on, not the bedrock ceiling.

### [World map and markers](https://cassonademod.com/features/world-map/)

Craft a world map from an empty map and eight sticks, and you get a full-screen map of every chunk you've explored, under a fog of war.

*   Right-click to place a named marker in any of the 16 dye colors. Markers show on the world map, the minimap and the direction bar.
*   Hold `Shift` over a place to see its biome and, once you've been to the Nether, where that spot leads through a portal; a panel lists the map's shortcuts.
*   A red X where you last died, until you get back there; the world's spawn chunk outlined; the lodestones your lodestone compasses point to.
*   **Ping** a spot or a mob with `Shift` + middle click: every player in the dimension sees it marked with your name for a few seconds.
*   Coordinates written in the chat become links that open the map there, ready to place a marker.
*   Your exploration is recorded from the very first minute, even before you craft the map.

### [Clock and barometer](https://cassonademod.com/features/clock/)

Equip a clock to see the time, the weather, the day count and the moon's phase, with a small bed and a soft chime when you can sleep. Combine it with a lightning rod and you get a **barometer**: it also shows the next weather change, read straight from the server's own weather timers.

### [Waystone](https://cassonademod.com/features/waystone/)

Fast travel, but only once you've "finished" the game, and only between waystones. You craft one from a world map on top of 5 chiseled stone bricks, under a chorus fruit, so it comes after reaching the End. Right-click it to list the other ones you placed or have been near, in every dimension, and click one to go there. Your own come first, and you can sort the list with arrows, like the server list. You can name it in its screen or at an anvil. Waystones also show on the world map as landmarks, but you can only travel from the block itself. It needs the mod on the server.

### [Stables](https://cassonademod.com/features/stables/)

Horses get lost so easily. Now you craft a hitching post from a lead, a wooden fence and a hay bale, or find one by the animal pens of some villages. Right-click it to open your stable: register up to 8 horses, donkeys, mules, llamas or camels, name them for free and see their speed, jump and health. Board one to keep it safe out of the world and take it out at any post, or call back the one you left far away, even if its chunk isn't loaded. Sneak and right-click to tie your horse to the post. It needs the mod on the server.

### [An inventory that keeps up](https://cassonademod.com/features/inventory/)

There's a lot here:

*   **Sort** a chest, a barrel, an ender chest, a Shulker box, your backpack or your inventory in one click, or with a middle-click.
*   **Stash** into the open chest the items it already holds.
*   **Search** a chest, a Shulker box or your backpack with `Ctrl+F`: names, enchantments and the contents of bundles and Shulker boxes.
*   **Shift-drag** across a row to move every stack you pass over.
*   **Scroll wheel**: scroll down over a stack to move its items one at a time.
*   **Lock slots** with `Alt`-click: sorting, stashing and Shift-click leave them alone, and `Q` won't throw them away.
*   **Rotate your rows** into the hotbar with `G`, so each row can hold a set: tools, building blocks, food and torches.
*   **Bundles**: drag one over items to fill it, or right-drag over empty slots to empty it.
*   **Shulker boxes** click like bundles: click stacks into a box, drag over several, or scroll over it to pick which stack comes out.
*   **Previews** of bundles, Shulker boxes, backpacks and filled maps on hover, and the hunger and saturation food restores. With food in hand, what it would restore blinks on the hunger bar.
*   **Tooltips that tell more**: what each enchantment does, the anvil penalty of a worked item, how many items a fuel smelts.
*   **Furnaces and brewing stands**: the time left, how much more the fuel will do, and the experience a furnace holds.
*   **Picked up**: what you pick up is listed on the right of the screen, and marked with a small golden dot in your inventory until you hover it.
*   **Hand refill** when the stack in your hand runs out.
*   **Durability warning** before an enchanted tool or armor piece breaks.
*   **Bulk crafting**: `Ctrl`-click a recipe to craft as many as your inventory allows.
*   **Chain crafting**: click a recipe missing something you could craft first, and the recipe book makes it from your inventory: logs into planks, planks into sticks.

### [Right-click harvest](https://cassonademod.com/features/right-click-harvest/)

Right-click a ripe crop to harvest and replant it: wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots, nether wart and cocoa. You get the same drops as breaking it, Fortune included, minus the seed that goes back in the ground.

### [Elytra slot](https://cassonademod.com/features/elytra-slot/)

Elytra get their own slot, next to the offhand, so you can fly without taking your chestplate off. They fly, wear out and take Mending exactly like in vanilla.

### [Filtered hopper](https://cassonademod.com/features/hopper-filter/)

Every hopper gets a filter slot: put one item in, and the hopper only accepts that item. Put a bundle or a Shulker box in the filter to let through every item it holds. A row of filtered hoppers makes a full item sorter, no redstone needed, so no PhD either.

### [Quest journal and advancements](https://cassonademod.com/features/quests/)

*   A quest journal in twelve chapters walks you from your first night to the toughest challenges, following Minecraft's own advancements.
*   The current quest shows under the minimap, with its progress.
*   A new advancements screen that actually tells you what's left for each one (biomes to visit, mobs to kill…).

### [Combat awareness](https://cassonademod.com/features/combat/)

Combine a compass with a sculk sensor and you get a **sculk compass**: health bars over the mobs you fight, a "!" when a mob starts chasing you, and an eye in the corner of the screen while anything is hunting you, even out of sight. The minimap shows where the mobs hunting you come from, and the other players around you.

### [Spyglasses](https://cassonademod.com/features/naturalist-spyglass/)

Every spyglass gets a round lens and a finer zoom with the scroll wheel. Combine one with a book and a sculk sensor to make a **naturalist's spyglass**, which reads what you're looking at: a horse's speed, jump and health, when animals can breed or grow up, a villager's level and sold-out trades, how far crops have grown, a beehive's honey. You can also find one, rarely, in shipwreck, ocean monument and pirate booty chests, and up in lighthouses.

### [Sculk torch](https://cassonademod.com/features/sculk-torch/)

Combine a torch with a sculk sensor and you get a **sculk torch**. Hold it in either hand: it lights up around you and shows every nearby block where monsters can spawn, at any time or only at night. Light those spots up and the marks go away.

### [The sea](https://cassonademod.com/features/the-sea/)

In vanilla the sea is a clear blue pool. I wanted a real sea, one you're a bit scared to dive into.

*   **From the shore**: a clean horizon the sun sets behind, far water turned opaque, and a surface that no longer repeats block by block.
*   **Under water**: the sky only overhead, dim and cloudy depths, and a short, blurred view, lost in the murk past about 30 blocks. It's fair: monsters don't see you any better through the water.
*   **Diving mask**: five leather around a glass pane, shaped like a helmet, worn instead of your helmet. Your view stays sharp and reaches twice as far, and its snorkel lets you breathe with your head up to about 2 blocks under the surface. Dive deeper and your last breath lasts 5 seconds. It takes a helmet's enchantments, Respiration and Aqua Affinity included.

### [Villages and treasures](https://cassonademod.com/features/villages/)

Exploring in vanilla rarely pays off: villages all look the same, and most chests hold bones and rotten flesh. So:

*   **Towns**: now and then, a village grown three times larger, its houses packed close along its streets, around a market square where the wandering trader sets up shop.
*   **Lamp posts**: lanterns on posts along village streets, instead of torches on a fence.
*   **Taverns**: churches become taverns or small inns, with a bar, kegs, a fireplace and a tavern keeper in waistcoat and apron. In the evening, villagers meet there instead of at the bell.
*   **Lighthouses**: rarely, on the coasts, a white tower with its keeper's cottage, a chest of supplies and often a spyglass up by the lamp. At night, far out at sea, you see its light flash as the beam turns toward you.
*   **Lone dwellings**: now and then, out in the wild, a hunter's cabin, a shepherd's hut, a hermit's home or a fisherman's shack on a river's bank, a villager living there. Some were left, cold and full of cobwebs.
*   **Miners' camps**: one or two to a mineshaft, a straw bed, a cold campfire and a barrel of the miners' things.
*   **Stone bricks**: village houses and woodland mansions built mostly of stone bricks, some mossy or cracked, instead of cobblestone.
*   **Treasures**: furnished mansions with richer chests, jungle temples with two more treasures per chest, and pillager outposts, desert pyramids and igloos with one more, barrels of treasure in the ocean monuments, and very rarely, a pirate's booty in a lush cave, a pile of barrels full of gold and gems, which a pirate's map found in shipwrecks leads to.

All of it with vanilla blocks and vanilla loot, in new chunks.

### [Woodcutter](https://cassonademod.com/features/woodcutter/)

The stonecutter's twin for wood, crafted from an iron ingot over three planks: put a plank in, pick stairs, slabs, fences, doors, trapdoors, signs, buttons or pressure plates of the same wood, and take them out without losing anything. Both cutters and the loom remember what you picked for the next stack.

### [A refined interface](https://cassonademod.com/features/interface/)

This is the web designer in me: sepia wood panels, dark slots and cream text across the HUD, the chat, inventories and tooltips; windows that rise gently into place over a softly blurred world; status effects with their time left; a recipe book that looks like a book, and an enchanting table that shows every enchantment an option will give.

*   A new title screen, with its buttons in a column over the panorama.
*   World and server selection in two panels: your list on one side, and on the other the details of the one selected.
*   While a world loads, your map draws itself: what you explored comes into color chunk by chunk, with a tip about the mod.
*   Plain backgrounds for every other menu, small dialogs in framed panels, and a death screen with a skull above its panel.

Available in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Chinese, Russian and Japanese.

## Fair recipes

Some vanilla recipes eat a whole block for a single button, which always felt wrong to me. I only changed how many items a recipe gives, never its shape or when you unlock it: 16 buttons or pressure plates, 24 wooden trapdoors, 32 sticks from 2 planks, 8 ladders, 8 stairs, 12 signs, doors or bowls, 8 chests or barrels, 6 paper from 3 sugar cane. Stairs and slabs craft back into full blocks without loss, wool back into its 4 string, a nether wart block into its 9 nether wart, and packed ice into its 9 ice.

## What Cassonade will never add

New ores, metals or gear tiers; magic, machines or tech trees; new mobs, biomes or dimensions; anything that makes the game easier to beat rather than nicer to play (a better reward late in the game, yes; a shortcut early on, no). Other mods do that very well, it's just not what this one is about.

## Requirements

*   Minecraft **26.3**
*   Fabric Loader **0.20.1** or later
*   **[Fabric API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api)**
*   Java **25**

**Multiplayer:** install the mod on the server and on every player's game. In singleplayer, there's nothing else to do. On a server without the mod, the game stays playable and everything that happens on your screen still works (the living world, lights, the interface, inventory tooltips and conveniences), but the equipment slots are not offered, so nothing worn works (backpack, minimap, world map, clock, elytra slot, combat awareness), and the mod's items, recipes, structures and rules are not there. [The full list](https://github.com/corentin-gautier/cassonade#on-a-server-without-the-mod).
