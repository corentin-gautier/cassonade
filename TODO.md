# To do

Features planned for Cassonade, in the spirit of the mod: nothing new to mine or fight, nothing that makes the game easier to beat, each feature unlocked by equipping a vanilla item or one crafted from vanilla items. A feature that needs the server stays hidden on a server without the mod.

When a feature ships, remove it from here and describe it in the [README](README.md).

## Planned

- **Silverfish and endermites easier to hit**: their tiny hitbox makes them slip under the crosshair. Same as baby zombies: a slightly larger pick radius, the real hitbox unchanged.

## To discuss

- **Rainbow after the rain**: opposite the sun as the rain stops. Smooth bands and then square pixels were tried and didn't look vanilla enough.
- **Village life moments**: more scenes like the tavern evenings, with no effect on the game: children playing on the square, farmers bringing their harvest to the market in the morning, a feast after a raid is beaten.
- **Mount and player information while riding**: vanilla's display is odd. To rethink as a whole: both players' and mounts' health, hunger and other data.

## Turned down

Kept here so they don't come back.

- **Notable places recorded on their own** (bed, portals): only the spawn chunk is kept, don't automatically do stuff without the player input.
- **Tamed animals immune to arrows**: just don't aim at them.
- **Plain backpack picking up items**: like a real one, it has to be opened to put something in; only the organized one tops up its own stacks.
- **Ender chest preview**: players keep ender chests everywhere, so it is rarely needed.
- **Chest under a block**: a real chest needs room to open.
- **Dropped items blinking** before despawning: too late is too late.
- **Saplings replanting themselves**.
- **Sticks from logs**: sticks are already 32 for 2 planks.
- **Sheep's color and wool regrowth** through the naturalist's spyglass: not interesting enough.
- **Last raiders on the minimap**: ringing a bell already makes nearby raiders glow.
- **Marker search** on the world map: no real use.
- **Death X countdown** before items despawn: when you die far away, the chunks aren't loaded anyway.
- **Hopper direction**: the spout already shows it.
- **Librarian's book** through the naturalist's spyglass: players build trading halls with signs.
- **Villager's workstation and bed** through the naturalist's spyglass: no real need behind it.
- **Beacon range**: too much, and beacons are meant to be rare.
- **Anvil cost before combining**: not interesting enough.
- **Anvil remembering the last name**: renaming many items the same way is rare.
- **Cats on chests** standing up: blocking the chest is a funny vanilla touch.
- **Lapis staying in the enchanting table**: better kept in the inventory.
- **Decorated pots' contents** on look: pots are meant to be broken, and nobody stores things in them.
- **Double fence gates** opening together: nobody builds them.
- **Right-click harvest for sugar cane and bamboo**: already fast to harvest.
- **Vanilla map banners on the world map**: rarely used, and the world map's markers already do the job.
- **Painting choice at the stonecutter or woodcutter**: the scroll wheel when placing it is what shipped.
- **Potion effect ending warning**: effects already show their countdown at the top of the screen.
- **Keeping a filled bucket from dropping** when the inventory is full: no real use.
- **Placing a block past the one you aim at, without sneaking**: adds complexity to placing blocks; safe bridging is what shipped.
- **Pattern preview in the loom** without a banner or dye: not that useful.
- **Villager death notice**: very niche, and noise for the player.
