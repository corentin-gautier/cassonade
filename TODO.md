# To do

Features planned for Cassonade, in the spirit of the mod: nothing new to mine or fight, nothing that makes the game easier to beat, each feature unlocked by equipping a vanilla item or one crafted from vanilla items. A feature that needs the server stays hidden on a server without the mod.

When a feature ships, remove it from here and describe it in the [README](README.md).

## Planned

Nothing yet

## Set aside

- **Search in containers**: a search field on paper, like the recipe book's, in chests, barrels, the ender chest, Shulker boxes and the backpack; slots that don't match darken. It matches names, ids, enchantments, and what Shulker boxes and bundles hold. Client only. Set aside because the title rows have little room for a field (sort buttons, long custom names): it needs the windows' layout rethought.

## To discuss

- **Phantom warning**: a "Zz" icon on the clock was too much, and most players sleep anyway. Maybe an icon only on the night phantoms can come (three days without sleep, read from the server's statistics).
- **Shared markers in multiplayer**: copying your world map passes its markers on. Good, but of limited use.
- **Ammo count** next to the bow or crossbow in the hotbar: useful, but easy to confuse with the stack count.
- **Double trapdoors** opening together: does anyone build them?
- **Feature toggles**: no settings screen, but some features may need a way to be turned off. To decide case by case.

## Turned down

Kept here so they don't come back.

- **Notable places recorded on their own** (bed, portals): only the spawn chunk is kept.
- **Nether ↔ Overworld coordinates** on the world map: confusing for players who haven't been to the Nether yet.
- **Tamed animals immune to arrows**: just don't aim at them.
- **Backpack picking up items**: like a real one, it has to be opened to put something in.
- **Ender chest preview**: players keep ender chests everywhere, so it is rarely needed.
- **Chest under a block**: a real chest needs room to open.
- **Dropped items blinking** before despawning: too late is too late.
- **Saplings replanting themselves**.
- **Sticks from logs**: sticks are already 32 for 2 planks.
