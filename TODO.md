# To do

Features planned for Cassonade, in the spirit of the mod: nothing new to mine or fight, nothing that makes the game easier to beat, each feature unlocked by equipping a vanilla item or one crafted from vanilla items. A feature that needs the server stays hidden on a server without the mod.

When a feature ships, remove it from here and describe it in the [README](README.md).

## Planned

- **Hunters on the minimap**: with the sculk compass, the mobs hunting you show as red dots on the minimap, so you know where they come from, not only that they are there. Uses the hunters the server already sends for the eye; hidden on a server without the mod, like the eye.
- **Hit through grass**: an attack aimed at a mob behind tall grass, flowers or other plants with no collision reaches the mob instead of breaking the plant.
- **Smart spyglass**: a new item crafted from a spyglass and a book (it reads what it looks at), worn in its own equipment slot. A key zooms without holding it, and a small panel tells what you are looking at: a horse's speed, jump height and health (strength for a llama), a villager's profession and whether it has restocked, whether an animal is ready to breed, a crop's growth, a beehive's honey. On a server without the mod, only the zoom is left. Needs room for a new slot in the gear column.
- **Repair cost** already piled up on an item, in its tooltip.
- **Signs on chests**: clicking a sign placed on a chest or barrel opens it, instead of editing the sign.
- **Picked-up items**: a discreet list on the right side of the screen of what you just picked up, with the counts merged, fading out after a few seconds. Kept clear of the minimap and the current quest. Client only.

## Set aside

- **Search in containers**: a search field on paper, like the recipe book's, in chests, barrels, the ender chest, Shulker boxes and the backpack; slots that don't match darken. It matches names, ids, enchantments, and what Shulker boxes and bundles hold. Client only. Set aside because the title rows have little room for a field (sort buttons, long custom names): it needs the windows' layout rethought.

## Ideas

- **Notable places recorded on their own**: bed, Nether portals walked through, spawn points.
- **Nether ↔ Overworld coordinates** on the world map, to place a portal.
- **Shared markers in multiplayer**: copying your world map passes its markers on.
- **Temporary ping**: aim at a spot or a mob and the other players see it marked for a moment.
- **Farmland** no longer trampled by the player.
- **Tamed animals** no longer hurt by their owner (sweeping edge, arrows).
- **Double trapdoors and fence gates** open together, like double doors.
- **Sleepless nights** on the clock, to know when phantoms will come.
- **Scroll wheel** moves items between the inventory and the open container.
- **Mod settings screen**, from the Options menu and Mod Menu.
